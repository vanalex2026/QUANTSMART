# QUANTSMART product tour

The current home screen focuses on one XAUUSD M5 candle chart. Pan, zoom or use
the drawing toolbar to inspect price action. **Show context** opens the measured
pattern census and PDF knowledge lens.

The complete retained price-action history scans in a background worker. While
**PATTERN HISTORY | SCANNING** is visible, statistics and PDF markers are pending;
the candle chart, pan/zoom and drawing controls remain available. If the scan
fails, **Retry pattern history scan** runs it again. A changed tape or symbol
discards old results. Imported occurrence selections resume by UTC after the
new scan completes; equal closed-bar polls reuse the completed scan.

The separate candle-analogue comparison also runs in a background worker.
**Comparing closed-candle analogues** keeps its preview and forecast pending
until the result is ready. Failed jobs expose **Retry candle analogue analysis**.
The existing 6,000-bar forecast scope and 60-match preview limit are unchanged;
the PDF occurrence census still scans the complete retained two-year tape.
A selected analogue UTC endpoint survives recomputation and snapshot resume.

In **Price action knowledge**, **Show details** opens 23 curated concept lenses
and a 12-chapter book index. Select a chapter to see printed/PDF page ranges,
reviewed pages and related lenses. Chapter 9 includes the March-August 2012 case
ranges and source annotation glossary. Coverage is partial: chapter navigation
does not mean every figure has been reviewed. Ten source-note lenses (including exit management) have no detector
statistics. Thirteen geometry proxies now include regular pattern break, its post-break
pullback and close-confirmed pattern-break combi; their measured history remains separate from the source notes.
Marker and inspector references show printed and PDF page numbers side by side.

Pattern-break-pullback inspection also shows **Pullback strength and turn
buildup**: correction depth, opposing bodies/powerbars, close speed, efficiency
and a compact terminal cluster. Its outlined mini-chart span ends at the signal
before confirmation. Source anchors and local measurement rules are visible;
these values describe the setup and do not filter its historical statistics.

**Pullback reversal** now uses its version3 EMA-tested dominant-leg/correction
structure. Select it in the census to browse the newly recomputed history. The
mini chart labels **A** (leg origin), **E** (directional extreme), **T** (correction),
**B** (turn buildup), **S** (signal), **C** (close confirmation), with the full local
leg visible when scrolled left. Its inspector shows retracement, relative bar
ranges, EMA seed and optional prior wick test. Earlier shape-only counts are
replaced; these are local research rules with explicit source-method limits.
For a measured second close break, **S1** marks the earlier EMA-tested signal,
**C1** its close break and **R** a separate opposing return; **S/C** then mark
the later signal/confirmation. The inspector distinguishes this local M/W
sequence from a first close break and retains the earlier times, edge and EMA.
Printed129 explains the sequence; printed130-131 explain why wider trend/range
context can still invalidate an attractive local turn. That range selection
is source guidance and has not been implemented as a detector filter.

Select **Pattern break combi** in the census to browse its complete measured
history. The mini chart marks **P** (powerbar), **I1/I2** (inside bars) and **C**
(close confirmation), with amber break and blue technical-protection references.
The inspector explains the measured structure and its local numerical rules.
These references are separate from the illustrative ATR LONG/SHORT position.
Wick-only breaks and confirmation bars touching both sides are excluded. The
source's doji/reversed variants are not yet implemented.

Select **Pattern break** for compact boundary buildup, with **B1...** (buildup),
**S** (signal bar) and **C** (subsequent close confirmation). Its measured receipt
shows context boundary, boundary-touch count, signal edge and opposite technical
protection. Printed73-77 / PDF83-87 explain the source. Numerical thresholds,
close confirmation and the ATR outcome study are local research choices;
wider obstacle room and discretionary context are not yet assessed.

Select **Pattern break pullback** for a second-instance setup after an original
break. **X** marks that break, **T** the post-break tests, **S** the new signal,
and **C** its close confirmation. The mini chart shows the old boundary and an
amber EMA25-at-signal dot; the inspector discloses its40-bar bounded estimate.
Hanging barrier fights and excursion/return tests are distinct measured variants.
These EMA25 measurements differ from the main chart's EMA10/34 display lines.

In the inspector, **Room ahead at setup close** compares known support/resistance
with the confirmation close and technical protection. A separate50-unit round
landmark is shown as context. **Context** / **Inspect wider context** opens up to
96 closed M5 candles ending at the setup, with amber confirmed swing zones;
scroll or use Left/Right to inspect them. Missing levels do not mean a clear path.
This optional context does not filter history or alter the ATR position study.

**Wider swing and range context** also shows the last two confirmed highs/lows,
their progression, bounded EMA25 change and close-path efficiency. **Inspect
swing and range context** opens the causal Context view: **H1/H2** mark highs,
**L1/L2** mark lows, and a repeated range has an amber outline with a blue floor.
The inspector flags a setup opposing both swing steps, an UP setup in the upper
quarter or a DOWN setup in the lower quarter of a measured range. Every swing
needs two later closed candles known by the setup; missing/gapped/unverified
context is withheld. Local numerical rules are visible and do not filter the
167 reversal occurrences or establish an entry. Snapshot context receipts are
informational and recomputed after resume.

Select a census row or chart pattern marker to open its candle window, explanation
and historical evidence. **Earlier**, **Later**, **First**, **Latest**, and the
**Occurrence** number field browse every measured occurrence in the retained
two-year tape. Previewing leaves the main chart in place; **Show this setup on
chart** moves it to the selected UTC candle and shows that pattern's marker.
Directional PDF occurrences also show a historical LONG/SHORT study: shaded
target and invalidation areas with fixed setup-close prices. The observed area
ends at the first barrier event, the 24-bar horizon, or the last usable closed
candle before a missing/invalid transition. Labels remain separate from candles.
Current-source landmarks appear only at the retained tail and yield to an
explicitly selected historical position. These historical levels illustrate the
study policy, rather than an executable order or a verified PDF strategy entry.
**Escape** closes the inspector. The optional historical marker layer shows only
the latest 80 occurrences; this does not limit the history available for inspection.

The reported next-bar direction hit rate measures a close-to-close outcome. It is
separate from a target-before-invalidation trade win rate. Check the source badge:
local historical fallback does not establish a verified live MT5 connection.

Named-candle markers now open every evaluable match of the selected candle shape
in the retained tape, without the analogue engine's 60-match limit or similarity
filters. Historical evidence shows the evaluated/excluded setup counts, observed
UP/FLAT/DOWN distribution and missing next-bar outcomes. The Atlas H+1 rule treats
moves within +/-0.12 setup ATR14 as FLAT. Missing outcomes remain browsable and
are excluded from the directional hit-rate denominator; neutral patterns have no
directional hit rate. These taxonomy statistics are separate from PDF setups and
context-based analogue forecasts. **Show this setup on chart** can display a named
occurrence even when it is outside the analogue sample, with an illustrative
LONG/SHORT study when its directional geometry is valid. Snapshots and bookmarks
retain this independent named-history focus by pattern name and UTC time. Resume
rechecks the shape against the current tape; expired or changed setups are reported
as unavailable without selecting a substitute. The inspector closes on resume.
Bookmarks store evidence summaries and rebuild history from current data. Full JSON
exports preserve historical rows in compact JSON so they fit the bounded importer.

The PDF inspector also includes a **Target / invalidation study**. Its illustrative
policy enters at the setup close, places target at 2.2 setup ATR and invalidation
at 1 setup ATR, then observes up to 24 consecutive closed M5 bars. Setup ATR is
the mean true range across the six intervals ending at that candle. Counts show
known first touches, same-candle ambiguity, timeouts, incomplete paths and gaps.
Only known first touches enter the displayed target-first rate. This OHLC study
does not model spread, fees, slippage, executable fills or independent trades.

The endpoint badge says **VIEW** while browsing history, **LAST** at the latest
retained closed candle and **LIVE** only when a forming candle is in view.
Both closed and forming candles use teal `#26a69a` up / red `#ef5350` down at full
brightness. A dashed edge and LIVE badge distinguish a forming context candle.

After inspecting a historical setup, **Reset**, manual pan and zoom stay in place
when the underlying data refreshes. **Show this setup on chart** can be pressed
again to return to the same selected UTC candle. Refreshing the retained two-year
window preserves visible candle identities instead of replaying the old jump.

In the inspector, **Setup** shows the candle context with up to four subsequent
closed M5 candles. **Outcome path** shows the entire observed illustrative study,
ending at its barrier event, 24-bar timeout, or last usable candle before missing
data. Missing/forming/invalid rows are excluded from both views. A dashed column
marks the last observation; a same-candle double touch stays explicitly ambiguous.
The mini chart scrolls horizontally with a stationary price axis. Hover or tap a
candle to read its UTC/OHLC, or focus the plot and use Left/Right, Home and End.
**End of path** brings the final observed candle into view. All these actions
leave the main chart unchanged until **Show this setup on chart** is pressed.

## Cockpit

### 1. Overview

The three-column Cockpit combines a price chart with six telemetry gauges,
multi-timeframe observations, data-quality diagnostics and Strategy Card controls.
Switch between historical data and a connected live feed, inspect the source time,
and run a selected Card without leaving the market view. Range rank and EMA gap are
descriptive measurements. A missing entry assessment remains unassessed.

![Full-screen Cockpit with price chart and Strategy Card controls](screenshots/cockpit-overview.png)

The former Entry & Danger summary page has been retired. Its old `#entry-danger`
links now open the focused XAUUSD M5 home chart.

## Microscope

### 3. Chart & Replay

Examine price action, import your own CSV, and replay a selected Card against
chronological history. Compare the design, execution and chart timeframes, inspect
individual events, and review long-history, OOS and holdout windows. Replay uses
modeled execution; check the recorded source, costs and fill assumptions.

![Full-screen Chart and Replay using historical fallback](screenshots/chart-replay.png)

The former standalone KIDI Events diagnostic page has been retired. Its old
`#kidi-events` links now open the focused XAUUSD M5 home chart. The shared
flat-feed witness remains available in legacy Chart & Replay diagnostics.

## Strategy Studio

The former standalone Playbook has been retired. Its old `#playbook` links now
open the focused XAUUSD M5 home chart. Dated research archives, shared instability
feature helpers and broker services are preserved.

### 6. Strategy Lab

Browse the Strategy Album, select a Card, inspect its module composition and review
saved validation evidence. Run or cancel an owned backtest from the adjacent controls,
then compare the result with the Card's current configuration. Changed designs make
older evidence stale; a Card's name alone does not establish that it was tested.

![Strategy Lab](screenshots/strategy-lab.jpg)

### 7. Canvas

Build your own quantitative strategy from reusable components. The module library,
composition lanes and inspector expose dependencies, roles and wiring. Name and save
a Card, validate its composition, and send it through the measured publication
workflow. Imported or example designs remain drafts until their checks succeed.

![Strategy Canvas](screenshots/strategy-canvas.jpg)

### 8. Blocks & Modules

Inspect primitive blocks, engine modules and custom compositions. Search the library,
compare roles and examine a component's mechanism before adding it to a strategy.
Module descriptions and impact warnings help distinguish a proposed hypothesis from
a contribution that has actually been measured in a combined strategy.

![Blocks and modules](screenshots/blocks-modules.jpg)

### 9. Beast

Compose role-based Card ensembles, inspect member lineage and compare simulated
matchups. The workspace also exposes environment-routing experiments and editable
starter compositions. A template or compatibility label is a research starting point;
its members and current definition must match the evidence being reviewed.

![Beast ensemble workspace](screenshots/beast-lab.jpg)

### 10. Coordinate Lab

Explore mathematical expressions through curves, heatmaps, slices and 3D surfaces.
Inspect modeled geometry before turning an idea into a reusable component. The
built-in formula example is labeled as an example; visual structure in a formula
plot is not evidence of predictive market performance.

![Coordinate Lab](screenshots/formula-lab.jpg)

## System

### 14. Hidden Coefficients

Audit configuration coefficients, their provenance and their calibration status.
Use numeric controls to edit a Shadow profile and distinguish configuration,
documented source constants and user overrides. Saving a profile changes the
research configuration; it does not authorize live tuning or trading.

![Hidden coefficients](screenshots/hidden-coefficients.jpg)

connectivity, current market data or live readiness.

### 16. Debugger

Inspect actual durable-workspace, research-engine or market-feed responses and
surface source errors directly. This is useful for tracing an unavailable panel
back to its API response. The screenshot uses the research-engine view so it does
not expose personal workspace payloads or broker account details.

## Suggested first experiment

For a closer look at the authoring workflow, open the illustrated
[Custom Module walkthrough](CUSTOM_MODULES.md).

1. Launch QUANTSMART and import a historical dataset you are authorized to use.
2. Open Canvas, assemble a small Card and inspect its dependencies.
3. Save and validate the Card, then run its backtest in Strategy Lab.
4. Replay individual observations in Microscope and compare validation coverage.
5. Change one hypothesis at a time and retain the source-linked receipts.

See the [README](../README.md) for installation, MT5 setup and the distinction
between research runs and optional manually confirmed Demo tickets.
